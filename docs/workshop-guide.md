# Which Target Workshop drill trains what

Every drill in the Target Workshop tab comes in an Easy and a Hard version (the Switch difficulty button moves between them). Start on Easy, move to Hard when you reach a silver medal. All sizes are in degrees of view, the unit the Tune screen shows. Use Tune to change size, speed or round length without leaving the drill.

| You want to improve | Play | What it does to you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flicking to a target and stopping on it | Gridshot 3x3, Gridshot 5x5 wide, Wall of six, Wide flicks, Speed clicks | Many targets, you choose the order. Wide flicks makes the flick long, Speed clicks makes it fast. |
| Switching between two enemies | Switch: Close pair, Far pair | Close pair trains short switches without overshooting, Far pair trains the long swing. |
| Tiny adjustments and patience | Precision: Micro ladder, Pinpoint, Long range | Small targets. Slow down, place the crosshair, then click. |
| Reacting to something that appears | Reaction: Blink, Pop-up, Light chain, Light scatter | A target shows up (or lights up) and leaves. Pop-up and the Light drills charge points for a target that gets away. |
| Hitting a strafing enemy | Strafe: Walk pace, Run pace, Full sprint, Counter-strafe stops | Real player speeds (2.97, 5.4, 6.75 m/s). Shoot at the stops. |
| Shooting only once you are still | Counter-strafe: Stop and shoot | The only drill where you move. Shots fired while you run go wide. Strafe to the next ball, stop, then shoot. |
| Distant, fast peekers | Peek: Long-range sprint peek | A small ball 40 m away that sprints and disappears after a short time. |
| Targets that jump or bounce | Jump: Jumping ball, Bounce: Zig-zag, Bounce: Wall rush | Wall rush ricochets off the edges like a billiard ball, so read the bounce. |
| Precision on a target that moves | Precision: Under movement | A small ball that swings slowly. Easy to follow, hard to hit exactly. |
| Holding the trigger and pulling recoil | Spray: Burst control (Phantom), Vandal control, Phantom control | One big ball with a lot of health. Hard has more health and a smaller, farther ball. |
| Smooth tracking (no button) | Tracking: Vertical, Depth, Orbit, Sway, Player strafe | Score is time with the crosshair on the ball. You never press a button in a tracking drill. |

## Notes by group

- **Reaction, Light chain and Light scatter.** The points are fixed places. In Light chain they light left to right and start over, so you can get into a rhythm. In Light scatter the order is shuffled (Hard lights two at a time). A point that goes dark before you shoot it costs points.
- **Tracking.** Vertical trains the direction most players track worst. Depth trains a ball that changes size and speed. Orbit trains a curve. Sway is a pendulum: you must slow down with the ball at each turn. Player strafe is the closest to tracking a real enemy: random stops, taps and turn-arounds.
- **Counter-strafe: Stop and shoot** uses the weapon's real movement inaccuracy, unlike the other drills (those are perfectly accurate while you stand still). If your shots land while you run, you are shooting too early.
- **Spray.** Hold the trigger. Short bursts are fine for Burst control, the two spray drills reward a long controlled pull.
- **Medals.** Every drill gives a score in points. After three runs of a drill the game shows Bronze (your median), Silver (your 80th percentile) and Gold (your best), the same for clicking and tracking drills. Tune changes can make runs not comparable, so edit the numbers in Routines, Medals if you want fixed targets.

## A 15-minute warm-up

1. Gridshot 3x3 (Easy), 1 minute: wake up the hand.
2. Wide flicks, 1 minute: long flicks.
3. Light chain, 1 minute: reaction.
4. Strafe: Run pace, 1 minute: moving targets.
5. Tracking: Sway or Player strafe, 1 minute: smooth control.
6. Spray: Vandal control, 1 minute: recoil.

Put these in a routine (Routines tab, the "+ Routine" button on each card) to run them in order.
