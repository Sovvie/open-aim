# Studio (Creator), round 2, for 1.6.0

## What players get
- **Snap and guide lines (2D).** Dragging a bot, your start, a route point, a trigger line end or an extra start now clicks onto a 0.5 m grid and lines up with the centre or edges of other things (and your start). A red dashed guide line with small diamonds shows what it lined up with. A **Snap** button in the header turns it off (remembered); **hold Alt** while dragging for free movement. Walls and boxes keep their own face-to-face snapping. With Snap off, dragging works as before (0.1 m steps).
- **"Timeline" panel** (button next to Preview moves). Lists in time order what each bot will do after the round starts, from your triggers: shows up with the round / after a delay / when you cross its line / swings after a kill / swings to a sound / trades another bot / takes its turn, plus smokes and "round ends". A bar shows where in the round it happens. Times that wait for you are "from X s": the earliest, if you run straight there and kill at once (it uses your start, the line or sound area, and the running speed). Red notes call out sequencing problems: something that never happens (a ring of trades, a swing with nobody in range, a line or sound area you cannot reach because "I can walk around" is off), something that cannot happen before the round ends, a long empty wait at the start. Click a row to select that bot; the selected bot's row also shows what it does after it comes out and when it comes back.
- **Drag and drop on the drill list.** Drop one or more `.openvaim-drill.json` files on the list: one opens as a new unsaved drill (like Import), several are saved straight into the list as new drills. Dropping a file anywhere else no longer makes the page navigate to it (in the editor it says where to drop it).
- **Export on every drill card** (download icon), and the editor's Export button now says "Export".
- **Arrow keys in the 3D view** nudge relative to the camera: up is away from the camera, down toward it, left and right as you see them (snapped to the nearest ground axis, so steps stay on the grid). Same steps as 2D (0.5 m, Shift 1 m, Alt 0.1 m), one undo step per run.

## Technical
- New pure modules: `app/studio/snap.js` (`extentOf`, `snapGuides`, `toGrid`, `cameraNudge`), `app/studio/timeline.js` (`dryRun(drill)`: rows, notes, end). `view3d.js` exposes `yaw()`. `studio.js`: `guideSnap()` called from `dragMove` (2D only; 3D drags keep their own standing-spot snapping), guide drawing in `paint`, Snap toggle (`localStorage studio-snap`), timeline panel (`refreshTimeline`, re-rendered only when the drill or selection changed), `readDrillFile`/`dropDrillFiles`/`exportDrill`. CSS appended to `studio.css`.
- No drill format change, no compiler change (`COMPILER` still 25); saved layouts untouched. The timeline reads the same fields the compiler reads and models: delay, trigger line (distance from start / run speed), swing radius (needs another bot with a spot within the radius, uses that bot's earliest time), sound area (distance from start to the box), trades (target's earliest + tradeMin, plus `tradeStart`), take-turns order (rough: about the round gap).
- Tests: `tests/test-studio-snap-timeline.cjs` (14 checks). `node tests/run-all.cjs`: 13 files green.

## First-time-creator review (browser pane, stubbed desktop API)
Walked: new drill (Peeks starter), tour, drag, undo, 3D, templates button, library, import/export, drop. Fixed or changed: header export was an unlabeled icon next to Save (now "Export"); the shortcut sheet did not mention snapping or Alt. Noticed and left alone: icon-only buttons on drill cards (copy, delete) rely on tooltips; the first-run tour card covers the Preview/Timeline buttons until skipped; dragging a peeker's icon moves only its first route point (existing behaviour: its peek spot stays), which surprised me as a new user but changing it would alter how routes are edited.

## Not verified / limits
- Not run in the real Electron app, no live round. Checked in the browser pane against `app/studio` with a stub of the desktop API: guide lines and snapping (simulated pointer events), the timeline with trigger/trade, library drop of two files plus a non-JSON file (simulated DataTransfer), 3D arrow nudge, header at 1100 and 1500 px. The Export file save dialog and the real file picker were not exercised.
- Timeline times are estimates (straight-line running, instant kills, no walls in the way); take-turns timing is approximate; copycat tracks, flashes and unpredictable peeks are not modelled in time.
- Grid is 0.5 m while Snap is on (0.1 m free drag stays on Alt or with Snap off); group drags snap the grabbed item and the rest follow by the same amount.
