# analytics2 workstream (1.6.0, round 2)

## What players get
- **Drill goals** (Drills tab, open a drill): "Reach Silver / Gold" (only tiers above your best medal), "Score N" (must be beyond your best; below it on a drill where less is better) and, for drills with line-of-sight tracking data, "Hold N% on the body" (over your newest 3 tracked rounds). Each goal has a progress bar and a status line, can be removed, and the drill's row in the list shows "goal 62%". Reaching a goal (checked after every finished run, at start and when the Drills tab is drawn) shows a short message in the same style as the "new best" note and keeps the goal marked as reached. Up to 6 goals per drill.
- **Suggested warm-up** (top of the Drills tab): built from the coach's "you start sessions cold" numbers and your most played drills (the ones you hit the most with, easiest first, the first one twice), shown with the reason. One click, "Add to Routines", saves it as a routine called "Suggested warm-up" (saving again updates the same routine).
- **Session tab: drills against last time.** Every drill of the session now shows its best score and a change against the last earlier session that had it: accuracy points and best-score change, green or red, "first time" when there is nothing to compare.
- **Fixes found by looking at the screens with real-looking data:**
  - the heatmap's drill filter showed share codes; it shows drill names now (a drill the gallery no longer lists keeps the name stored with its runs);
  - Compare: an arrow pointed up for changes that count as "about the same"; it points sideways now. Compare no longer depends only on the day totals: a day with no summary row (or one that lags the stored rounds) is read from the rounds, so "Nothing recorded in the last 14 days" cannot appear while rounds exist;
  - the drill score chart was huge on wide windows (it scaled to the full width); it is capped and the three medal lines are labelled Bronze, Silver and Gold;
  - at phone width the seven Stats tabs wrapped into three ragged rows (now two even rows), and drill names were cut to three letters (they wrap to two lines now);
  - "0% headshots" on drills whose targets have no head is no longer shown.

## Technical
- Files: `app/analytics-ui.js`, `app/coach.js` (`warmupPlan`, `shapeOf`, constants `WARMUP_*`), tests `tests/test-analytics-views.cjs` (9 new tests), `tests/test-coach.cjs` (1 new). `routines.js` untouched: the warm-up uses `__aimRoutines.saveRoutine` and `.routines()`.
- Goals: localStorage `aim-an-goals-v1` = `{ [drill code]: [{ id, type: "medal"|"score"|"body", tier|target|percent, since, base, done }] }`. Pure functions `normalizeGoal(s)`, `addGoal`, `removeGoal`, `evaluateGoal` (exposed under `__aimAnalyticsUi.render`), `checkGoals()` (on `aim-routines-change`, which routines.js fires on every finished run, debounced 1.5 s; also 6 s after start and when Drills is drawn). Medal goals use the `medal` stored on each run since the goal was set; score goals the best finished run since; body goals `roundFacts` tracking ticks of rounds since (needs sight data and 3 rounds). A run that ended in death never counts. The toast reuses the theme's `.es-score-toast.is-good`.
- Warm-up: `__aimCoach.warmupPlan(rounds)` returns `{ cold, gapPoints, firstAccuracyPercent, middleAccuracyPercent, sessions, steps: [{ code, runs, rounds, accuracyPercent }], reason }`; candidates need 3 rounds, 60 shots and under 30% of rounds ending in death. `sessionShape` now goes through the same `shapeOf` (unchanged results, tests green).
- Session deltas: `sessionDrillChanges(sessions, index, runsState, nameOf)`, looks back 12 sessions; accuracy needs 15 shots in both sessions, 2 points counts as same; score within 2% counts as same.
- Compare data: `mergeRawDays(days, facts)`; day rows are never lowered.
- How I checked visually: ran this worktree with `electron . --port=8151` on the second monitor, seeded `aim-analytics` through the recorder's own legacy-key migration path (300 rounds over 50 days, 6 drills incl. a tracking drill, rounds without sight data older than 21 days, a drill the gallery does not list, bots drills with deaths; days older than 30 are rolled up by the real roll-up) plus medal runs through `logic.recordRun` and two routines, then opened every Stats tab at 1500, 1100 and 375 px in the browser pane. Cross-checked: Session tile (7 rounds, 382 shots, 54%, 46 kills), Compare (40 rounds / 2491 shots against 38 / 2019, deaths 3 against 6) against the stored rows. Goal completion was exercised by finishing a run through `__aimRoutines.finishRun`.

## Not verified / limits
- **Compare "Nothing recorded in the last 14 days" could not be reproduced**: with day rows written by the recorder it shows the numbers. The only path to that message with rounds present is missing or lagging day rows (a failed roll-up), which `mergeRawDays` now covers. If it happens again, check `__aimAnalytics.days({})` in the page: if it is empty while `rounds({})` is not, the roll-up did not run.
- Not checked live in a round: the goal message over a real result screen (it uses a fixed-position element like the leaderboard note), the result-screen hit report was rendered with `hitPanelHtml` into a panel in the browser pane (heat glow and "How you fire" block look right), not on a real result screen. The body goal was only tested with synthetic rounds. The score-goal input and Enter key were driven by script, not typed.
- The browser pane is shared with other workstreams; tabs may be navigated away by others.
- Ideas: a goal that repeats ("again next week"), a goal suggestion from the trend, warm-up steps with minimum medals, showing goals on the lobby drill cards.
